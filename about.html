ABOUT THE OPERATOR

One creature.
Many faces.

I’m Izzy — an aspiring cybersecurity enthusiast who learns by building, testing, documenting, and helping other people navigate the internet more safely.

01 / THE DIRECTION

Security with a purpose.

I’m working toward a career in IT and cybersecurity. I’m especially interested in practical defense, privacy, systems, and penetration testing.

02 / THE FOUNDATION

Learning by doing.

My experience includes CompTIA Security+, CyberPatriot work with Windows Server and Linux, Active Directory fundamentals, and volunteer cybersecurity education.

03 / THE COMMUNITY

Share what you learn.

Through volunteer work, I’ve helped with CTF activities, student cohorts, presentations, demonstrations, and community talks on varying topics.

04 / THE PHILOSOPHY

Stay curious. Stay useful.

I don’t want to cosplay as a hacker or pretend to know everything. I want to keep learning, make useful things, test security measures, and leave people with something practical they can use.

THE MOTIVE / 01

Make the technical
feel human.

I’m drawn to cybersecurity because it combines investigation, problem-solving, and real-world impact. Good security should help people feel more capable—not make technology feel more intimidating.

CURRENT FOCUS / 02

Build. Document.
Repeat.

  • Build practical projects and document the decisions behind them.
  • Keep developing Linux, networking, systems, and defensive/offensive security skills.
  • Translate online-safety lessons into language anyone can use.
