THE PERSONAL MANIFESTO

Question the system.
Not your curiosity.

A few principles for how I want to learn, build, and show up in cybersecurity.

Understand how things work — including how they fail.

Use that knowledge responsibly. Build skills that protect people, respect boundaries, and make technology a little less hostile.

01 / Learn openly

Document the process. Share the useful parts. Be honest about what is still a work in progress.

02 / Test responsibly

Practice in labs and authorized environments. Curiosity is not an excuse to cross someone else’s boundaries.

03 / Keep it human

Security is about people, not just machines. Explain clearly, stay humble, and help others feel more capable.

04 / Make things

Read the docs, get hands-on, break your own setup, and build something that proves the lesson stuck.

05 / Question hype

Ignore the fake guru routine. Measure progress by real understanding, not how intimidating the terminal looks.

06 / Leave it better

Share what you can, help where you’re able, and aim for work that makes someone’s digital life safer.

People should know how to attack to defend.

And know when not to.
